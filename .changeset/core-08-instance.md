**BREAKING** — `createI18n(options)` returns the 0.8 instance: synchronous construction (no `init()`), exactly thirteen members — `locale`, `dir`, `ready`, `t`, `ns`, `setLocale`, `load`, `add`, `has`, `on`, `rev`, `destroy`, `setDefaultParams` — over one immutable state that `on()` subscribers are told about on every change. Options: `locale`, `fallbackLocale` (one, a list, or lists per locale with a `default`), `messages` (the `default` namespace) and `catalogs` (by namespace), `loader(locale, ns, signal)`, `namespaces`, `defaultParams`, `plugins`, `timeZone`, `onError`, `missing`. `t(key, { ns, locale, ...params })` switches namespace and locale per call; `i18n.ns("shop")` binds a namespace; the `"ns:key"` spelling is gone. A locale falls back through its parents (`pt-BR` → `pt`) and then `fallbackLocale`; an empty translation counts as missing. Catalogs are flat and kept by reference; `add()` over an existing catalog writes a patch layer instead of copying it. Loads are deduplicated per locale and namespace, and `setLocale()` switches inside the call when nothing has to load, otherwise once its loads settle — a namespace that fails to load (a 404 for one file) no longer blocks the switch; only a locale with no catalog at all rejects. Malformed locales are reported as `invalid_locale` before anything renders or loads. Every problem is an `onError` report with a code: `missing_key`, `missing_param`, `syntax`, `load_failed`, `invalid_locale`, `fallback_used`. New subpaths: `@comvi/core/cdn` (`cdn(base)` reads the Comvi CDN exactly as `@comvi/plugin-fetch-loader`'s CDN loader did, `withFallback` serves bundled catalogs), `@comvi/core/plugins` (`detect()` picks the start locale from the URL, cookie, storage or browser; `persist()` saves every switch), `@comvi/core/format` (`formatNumber`, `formatCurrency`, `formatDate`, `formatDateRange`, `formatRelativeTime`, `formatList`, `displayName`) and `@comvi/core/devtools` (`plugins: [devtools()]` makes the instance discoverable by the Comvi browser extension and loads the in-context editor on request, as before). `@comvi/plugin-fetch-loader` and `@comvi/plugin-locale-detector` are superseded by `/cdn` and `/plugins`.
