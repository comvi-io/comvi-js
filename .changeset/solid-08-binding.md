**BREAKING** — `@comvi/solid` is rebuilt on the 0.8 core. `<I18nProvider i18n loading?>` provides the instance (read once); `loading` replaces the children for the instance's first paint only, while a namespace is loading with no catalog usable for the locale, and never on a server or in a hydration. `useI18n(ns?)` returns `t`, `T`, `locale`, `dir`, `isReady`, `isLoading`, `setLocale` and the instance: `locale`, `dir`, `isReady` and `isLoading` are accessors, and what reads only `locale` or `dir` does not re-run when a namespace loads. `ns` binds `t` and `T` (one component per namespace; an `ns` prop never re-targets it) and is held from mount to cleanup: it loads for the locale on screen and every switch, a remount does not fetch a namespace whose load failed again (`i18n.load(ns)` retries). `<T i18nKey ns? locale? markers? components? {...params}>` renders a message and loads nothing: a tag renders through a handler prop of its name, its `components` entry (a tag name, a component or `{ component, props }`; an empty tag passes no children, so `<br/>` and `<br></br>` render a real `<br>` on the client and the server), else as text; typed by generated catalogs. It re-renders on a change of its props, the locale or its namespace's catalogs, not on a hold or another namespace's load. `useFormat()` from `@comvi/solid/format` formats numbers, currency, dates, ranges, lists, display names and relative time through the real instance and the locale on screen, re-running on a switch. On a server, an instance per request: `useI18n(ns)` holds at once, and a `<Suspense>` boundary above it waits through `createResource` until the catalog is in, in `renderToStringAsync` and in a stream; `handover(i18n)` is re-exported, and `createI18n({ ...options, ...payload })` in the browser hydrates without a mismatch and requests nothing the server rendered. The package has a `development` export condition: a development build explains `useI18n()` outside a provider; the production build ships no diagnostic text. Removed with the 0.7 core: `useI18nLoader`, `useI18nPlugins`, the `create*Signal`/`create*State` primitives, `localeSource`, `useI18nInstance`, `tRaw`, `isInitializing`, `isInitialized`, `cacheRevision`, `defaultParams`, the instance-method pass-throughs and the formatters on `useI18n()` (→ `@comvi/solid/format`); the provider's `autoInit`, `fallback` (→ `loading`), `ssrLocale` and `onError`; `<T>`'s `params`, `fallback` and children as a fallback; `{ tag, props }` entries (→ `{ component, props }`); the root re-exports of `icuCompiler`, `attachLoader`, `loader`, `definePlugin`, `plugins`, `devtools` and the four formatters.
